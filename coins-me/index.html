01 — The Challenge

The core experience was designed around three key product areas: saving in dollars, investing in USDC, and spending through one app.

The core experience was designed around three key product areas: saving in dollars, investing in USDC, and spending through one app.

From financial complexity to intuitive interactions: the product had to make digital money feel ordinary, not intimidating.

02 — The Process

Designing beyond individual screens.

We designed the key app screens across the complete user journey — from accessing balances and financial information to making payments and exploring investments.

To keep the product consistent and scalable, we created a reusable UI design system alongside the screens.

The app was designed in both dark and light themes, giving the product flexibility without compromising consistency.

03 — The Result

Extending the product language beyond the app.

One product. Two visual experiences. We developed a colour palette that could work seamlessly across both dark and light interfaces.

Large typography establishes emphasis, while smaller levels organise supporting information without creating visual noise.

Alongside the app interface, we created Instagram and social media designs to communicate coins.me’s features and product value.
