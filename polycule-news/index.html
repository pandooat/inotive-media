01 — The Challenge

Create a visual identity that feels credible enough for news, but fresh enough for a new generation of readers.

Create a visual identity that feels credible enough for news, but fresh enough for a new generation of readers.

02 — The Process

Designing beyond individual screens.

Clarity in every story. We built the identity around readable typography and a structure that scales from a headline to a full page.

Bold. Connected. Intelligent. The system is designed for screens, stories, and everything between.

Connection became the organising idea: how stories relate, and how readers move between them.
