Website Design

One USDC experience.

P2P.me is a peer-to-peer USDC platform that lets users buy, sell and pay with digital dollars using familiar local payment methods like UPI, PIX and QRIS.

Timeline

01 — The Challenge

Making crypto payments feel more familiar.

Making crypto payments feel more familiar. The platform needed to introduce itself without leaning on crypto jargon.

The landing page had to serve users who know digital dollars and users who only know their local payment method.

02 — The Process

Designing beyond individual screens.

We designed a landing page that introduces the platform, its value, and its ecosystem in one pass.

The page leads with familiar methods — UPI, PIX and QRIS — so the product feels local before it feels technical.

Visual design kept the interface quiet so the value proposition carries the page.
